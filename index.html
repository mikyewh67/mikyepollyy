Data & limits

Odds are implied prices, not independently verified win probabilities. A 100% sum check is not proof of an edge. Confidence describes data quality only. True win probability and positive expected value cannot be established from Gamma prices alone. Tracker entries and goals are local; clearing your browser data removes them unless exported.

Sports markets from polymarket.com may differ from markets offered on polymarket.us. Read every contract's settlement rules and confirm availability and pricing before taking any action.
